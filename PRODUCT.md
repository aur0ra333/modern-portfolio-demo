# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, interviewers, and technical reviewers who need to quickly understand Shen Haotian's background, project quality, and practical frontend/backend ability. They usually scan the page under time pressure before deciding whether to open GitHub links, online demos, or ask project questions in an interview.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents a 2026 computer science graduate through a small set of online projects that can be opened, inspected, and discussed. Success means the visitor can identify the strongest projects first, understand what each project demonstrates, and leave with a clear sense that the work is organized, credible, and not template-generated.

## Brand Personality

Clear, restrained, practical. The tone should feel like a careful junior developer who can explain decisions and finish details, not a marketing page trying to look bigger than it is.

## Anti-references

Avoid glassmorphism, oversized gradients, generic SaaS hero sections, fake dashboard decorations, repeated icon-card grids, vague AI-generated copy, cream/beige template palettes, and portfolio pages that only list links without explaining project value.

## Design Principles

- Lead with evidence: every project card should explain what can be opened, tested, or discussed.
- Make hierarchy obvious: primary projects come before supporting exercises.
- Stay specific: describe actual features, data flows, and interactions instead of generic praise.
- Keep visual noise low: use spacing, typography, and contrast before decoration.
- Make scanning easy: recruiters should understand the page in under one minute.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for body text and controls. Support keyboard-readable links and buttons, responsive layouts from mobile to desktop, and reduced-motion preferences for reveal or hover effects.
